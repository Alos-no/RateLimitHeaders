Remarks

This parser supports the IETF standard format:

  • RateLimit: "policy";r=remaining;t=reset_seconds
  • RateLimit-Policy: "policy";q=quota;w=window_seconds

Multiple comma-separated policies are supported per the spec: 

RateLimit: "burst";r=50;t=30,"daily";r=900;t=43200
When multiple policies are present, policy names are matched between headers and the most restrictive one (lowest remaining percentage) is returned.

Per the specification, malformed headers are silently ignored (never throws).
